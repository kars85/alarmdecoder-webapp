SSL Certificate Upload

Please upload the SSL certificates required to connect to the AlarmDecoder’s encrypted ser2sock service. These include:

  • CA Certificate: The Certificate Authority file that was used to sign the server's certificate.
  • Client Certificate: The client certificate for this web application.
  • Client Key: The private key corresponding to the client certificate.

All fields are required to establish an encrypted connection.
