AlarmDecoder Settings

Finally, configure the AlarmDecoder device itself. Most settings can remain at their defaults, but the Keypad Address should be set to an address enabled on your alarm panel (unique to this device).

For Honeywell **SE** panels, use address 31. For other Honeywell/Ademco panels, use any free address (after enabling it on the panel). Refer to your alarm panel documentation or consult your alarm provider to ensure the address is available for the AlarmDecoder.

Tip: For some Honeywell/Ademco panels, clicking "Get Panel Info" can retrieve the installer code and more (for panels supporting AUI protocol). If unsupported, you will be notified.
